Premierement, Animal. Parole plus sombre...Plus morbide.

[Couplet 1]

I cannot control myself
Like an insane animal
I search for an innocent victim
For fresh meat, fresh blood
I play the role of the death
You still alive or you die

Pay attention as you
Check behind yourself
Perhaps someone is following you

I walk silently on the road
Waiting for someone
A person from whom I can take his bones
An eat his flesh

Eyes, Brain, Heart… Decadence

To exterminate
Decapitate for my own fun

Paid attention as you

                                              SHE

[Couplet 1]

My life is still incomplete
Until I can talk to her
If she laugh at me
What can I do against the world?
Chorus

If I say my secret
Will she think its real (real)
If I say, I love you
I hope she believes me (Me!)

[Couplet 2]

The sorrow inside my blood
Destroys me at all
She looks at me
And laughs to finish me (Kill me!)

[Couplet 3]

The old man says
That love is blind
It is the truth if I don't open my eyes
And realize that what I refuse to see
(REFUSE TO SEEE)

Pain is suffering
I love her
Now I'm gone

[Chorus 2x]

Finalement, Six Feet Under. Ce sont des paroles encore une fois plus sombre. L'inspiration était au rendez-vous. Ca a rapport aux événement quotidien... M'enfin a vous de voir
Six feet under

Six feet under pressure

So I leave this place

Chorus
