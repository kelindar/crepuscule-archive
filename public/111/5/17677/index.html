Post by Xandek - November 11, 2007 at 9:14 PM

Battle Focus c'est Méditation version Warrior

Tiré de Stratics :

Battle Focus skill could be defined as the "Warrior's Meditation Skill." It increases both a player's Mana and Stamina regeneration. Since a warrior's swing rate is determined by the amount of stamina he has, the ability to increase the regeneration of that attribute would be highly beneficial on the battlefield. Mana, too, is an important stat to any warrior that uses special combat moves as part of his tactics.

Hit Points, Stamina, and Mana all have stats associated with them called "10 second gains," which are set on all creatures and tell the game system how much to increase each current value by every 10 seconds. This can be seen in the way monsters regenerate their stats points over time. These stats are usually set once and never change. However, in Age of Shadows, these 10-second gains can now be increased by various skill and magic effects, like the Battle Focus skill.

For every 10 points of Battle Focus a player has, their 10 second Stamina gain is increased by 1.
For every 20 points of Battle Focus a player has, their 10 second Mana gain is increased by 1.

Personnelement, Focus est Très utile pour ce qu'il amène.

Post by Xandek - November 11, 2007 at 11:40 PM

La grosse différence :

Méditation Offre un passif et un Actif en regen de mana.
Le Hic c'est que le Passif de regen Mana embarque plus dès que tu as une studded leather ou Plus.

En regen passif, à 100 Intel et 100 meditation avec une armure qui permets la méditation, un player regagne 1.3 mana / seconde (donc 13 mana / 10 secondes)

En Regen Actif, ca frôle le 3 mana / seconde (30 mana / 10 secondes), il ne faut pas porter d'armure ( en dehors de Leather) pour l'activer

Battle Focus Lui t'offre un regen Stamina ET Mana, peu importe l'armure que tu portes.

Stamina qui se calcule comme etant un + 10 / 10 secondes
Mana qui se calcule comme + 5 / 10 secondes

Considérant que les Warriors ont besoin de plus grosses armures, Focus offre un passif intéressant quand même (1 mana au 10 secondes / 20 points de skills) surtout puisqu'il s'ajoute avec meditation.

Donc, un perso avec 100 Focus, 100 Intel et 100 Meditation regen 1.8 mana / seconde (18 / 10 secondes)
