Post by Feydanith, dit la Carpe - April 21, 2007 at 5:33 PM

Actuellement :

  • Sur quelqu'un, détermine s'il appartient à la guilde des voleurs.
  • Sur un corps humain, détermine qui l'a tué.
  • Sur un corps non-humain, détermine qui l'a looté.
  • Sur une serrure, détermine qui l'a ouverte ( via lockpicking ).

Je regarde un peu comment ça fonctionne.

EDIT :

\t\tprivate Mobile\t\tm_Owner;\t\t\t\t// Whos corpse is this?
\t\tprivate Mobile\t\tm_Killer;\t\t\t\t// Who killed the owner?
\t\tprivate bool\t\tm_Carved;\t\t\t\t// Has this corpse been carved?

\t\tprivate ArrayList\tm_Looters;\t\t\t\t// Who's looted this corpse?
\t\tprivate ArrayList\tm_EquipItems;\t\t\t// List of items equiped when the owner died. Ingame, these items display /on/ the corpse, not just inside
\t\tprivate ArrayList\tm_Aggressors;\t\t\t// Anyone from this list will be able to loot this corpse; we attacked them, or they attacked us when we were freely attackable

\t\tprivate string\t\tm_CorpseName;\t\t\t// Value of the CorpseNameAttribute attached to the owner when he died -or- null if the owner had no CorpseNameAttribute; use "the remains of name"
\t\tprivate bool\t\tm_NoBones;\t\t\t\t// If true, this corpse will not turn into bones

\t\tprivate bool\t\tm_VisitedByTaxidermist;\t// Has this corpse yet been visited by a taxidermist?
\t\tprivate bool\t\tm_Channeled;\t\t\t// Has this corpse yet been used to channel spiritual energy? (AOS Spirit Speak)

\t\t// For notoriety:
\t\tprivate AccessLevel\tm_AccessLevel;\t\t\t// Which AccessLevel the owner had when he died
\t\tprivate Guild\t\tm_Guild;\t\t\t\t// Which Guild the owner was in when he died
\t\tprivate int\t\t\tm_Kills;\t\t\t\t// How many kills the owner had when he died
\t\tprivate bool\t\tm_Criminal;\t\t\t\t// Was the owner criminal when he died?

\t\tprivate DateTime\tm_TimeOfDeath;\t\t\t// What time was this corpse created?

Y'a ça à disposition, je suppose qu'on peut rajouter des attributs sans trop de risque que de détruire les corps déjà présent au sol.
