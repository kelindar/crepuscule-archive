# Crepuscule Forum Archive

## About Crepuscule

Crepuscule was a French-language medieval fantasy MMORPG server and roleplaying community based on a modified version of Ultima Online. Set in the world of Enrya, on an island called Systéria, it provided a rich environment for text-based roleplaying, character development, and community interaction between 2005 and approximately 2012.

The game featured:

- A D&D-inspired alignment system
- Multiple playable races and ethnicities
- A unique class system with five main branches:
  - Warriors (paladins, archers, monks, knights, rangers, samurai)
  - Rogues (thieves, spies, assassins, bards)
  - Mages (necromancers, druids, sorcerers, demonologists, summoners)
  - Artisans (tailors, blacksmiths, carpenters, alchemists, cooks)
  - Spiritualists (priests, enchanters, telepaths, scholars)
- Guild systems
- A complex character creation and development process
- Community-driven roleplay and storytelling

## About This Archive

This repository contains a static website archive of the Crepuscule forum, preserving the discussions, guides, and community content from this once-active roleplaying community. The archive serves as a historical reference for former players and those interested in French-language roleplaying communities.

The archive preserves the structure and content of the original forum, including:

   - Armée des Mercenaires (Mercenary Army)

   - Association des Commerçants (Merchants' Association)

3. **Key Documentation**

   - Community rules and standards

- **HTML5/CSS3** for structure and presentation

## Navigation

The archive maintains the original forum structure:

- Main categories are accessible from the homepage
- Sub-forums are organized hierarchically
- Individual threads display all posts with author information and dates

## Historical Context

Crepuscule was part of a vibrant era of text-based roleplaying communities in the mid-2000s to early 2010s. It represented a unique French-language contribution to the roleplaying scene with its detailed world-building, complex character development systems, and active community management.

The community was known for:

- Detailed lore about the world of Enrya
- Strict roleplaying guidelines that encouraged immersive storytelling
- A system of guilds with unique identities and internal hierarchies
- Community events and staff-led storylines
